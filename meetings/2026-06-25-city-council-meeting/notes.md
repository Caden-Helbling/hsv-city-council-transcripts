## TL;DR

- **All action items passed**; the lodging-tax ordinance (19A) was postponed a second
  time to July 9, pending the County Commission's TIF 9 vote.
- **Both "Private Park and Pay Facilities" items (19B action + 21G introduction,
  sponsor Kling) were withdrawn** — the parking-regulation ordinance is off the table
  for now.
- **47-acre Swancott Rd rezoning to denser R-2 approved** despite a resident's
  process questions — staff framed it as affordable-housing density in the
  Mazda-Toyota growth corridor.
- **NCAC director publicly asked the city to renegotiate the police SVU lease**
  toward market rate (~$14/sq ft now vs ~$25 market).
- Jim Batson's Gas Utility Board reappointment briefly drew a **"no" from Meredith
  on the 4/5-required roll call; he flipped to aye moments later** to make it unanimous.
- New City Hall park (Big Spring Park expansion): on schedule, ribbon cutting
  ~March–April 2027, before Panoply.

## Discussion highlights

**Swancott Rd rezoning (9b).** Resident Joy Johnson asked which district is denser
and why/how zoning changes after annexation. Planning manager Thomas Nunez: R-2 is
denser (side setbacks drop ~4 ft per side vs R1-B's 10 ft); process runs ~9 months
with 500-ft neighbor notification and Planning Commission first. The parcel sits in
a ~350-acre mixed-housing development in the western growth corridor (Mazda Toyota,
Amazon); staff and Robinson tied the density to affordable-housing goals.

**NCAC presentation (7a).** Director Chris Newland pitched the center's long-term
economic/public-health impact (child-maltreatment survivors without services earn
15–21% less; 9% of Medicaid costs tied to maltreatment). Concrete ask: HPD's entire
Special Victims Unit occupies ~3,600 sq ft on the NCAC campus at ~$14/sq ft with
NCAC covering all utilities/janitorial — roughly half the ~$25 downtown market rate.
He asked Council and Mayor to **reconsider the lease toward market value**. NCAC
currently gets $50K/yr in outside-agency funding.

**Green Mountain fire truck (20b).** Hamilton: the volunteer fire department raised
~$110K and is donating it toward a smaller, more maneuverable truck that carries its
own water (long driveways, wildland fires); fleet capital covers the remainder, and a
permanent station on the mountain is a future need. A councilmember (Meredith's
district covers Green Mountain — attribution uncertain in transcript) said he'll
divert **$30K of council improvement funds to fleet** for it at the next meeting.

**Big Spring Park / new City Hall park (Kling + Hamilton).** On schedule; ribbon
cutting ~March–April 2027 (before Panoply). ADA switchback path ("our version of
Lombard Street") doubles as an art walk with several installations. Structural work
now; finish work visible over the next ~45 days.

**Patton Road garbage (Kling, echoed by public comment).** Recurring overflow at
apartment buildings (3607/3609/3611 Patton Rd) across from the sportsplex; Kling was
bounced between HPD, inspection, community development, and public works and wants a
**defined policy for which department owns cart/dumpster violations**. Jerry Cox
(public comment) said six days of city contacts produced nothing; he'll escalate to
the county health department.

**Other member notes.** Watkins: 240+ kids at the Trevor Lacey basketball camp —
first event at the new John Hunt Park rec center; Junefest 10th anniversary weekend
(T.I. at the Orion, car show, kids' zone); free health fair at Johnson Legacy Center
Sat 9–12. Little: praise for the rec center/pickleball courts and sports tourism.
Robinson: June 27 semiquincentennial (250th) celebration at Ditto Landing ($5/car,
fireworks 9 PM); **Tennessee River Greenway closed at the event-center construction
June 29 → ~Sept 1**; kudos to City Clerk Shaundrika Edwards for the revamped
municipal-election qualifying process. Mayor Battle: July 4 / 250th events at
huntsvilleal.gov. Meredith: no comments.
