## TL;DR

- **Salty Bottom Road railroad crossing:** after a father (36) and his 8-year-old
  daughter were killed there last month — and with the victims' family in the
  chamber — a resident asked Council to work with ALDOT to add lights and gates
  at the unprotected crossing (2,100+ vehicles/day). No formal response recorded.
- **Demolition of 3804 Melody Rd NE approved over the owners' objection**, but with
  a ~30-day window in which the family may send a licensed contractor in to argue
  the house is salvageable before Public Works mobilizes.
- **The 1% lodging-tax increase for VBC capital improvements was postponed to
  June 25** so it lands the same night as the related TIF vote; if passed it would
  take effect in October.
- **$2,315,000 contract awarded (consent-adjacent, held item 20g) for the Ditto
  Landing event-space parking lot** — 450 spaces supporting the county's new event
  center; came in under the ~$3M budget.
- **A study of a possible new I-65 interchange (between exits 340 and 347) was
  funded** — pitched as a ~$30–40M alternative to a $300–400M rebuild of the
  congested I-65/I-565 interchange, serving the Mazda Toyota growth corridor.
- **Electric Utility Board seat turned competitive:** Watkins floor-nominated
  Samuel T. Greene II; Meredith then withdrew his nomination of Daniel Tate.
  Vote June 25.
- **Hearing set July 23 on a 505-acre Planned Development – Housing rezoning**
  (north of Bob Wade Ln at Mt. Lebanon Rd, "Harris Farms").

## Discussion highlights

**Melody Rd demolition (9a).** LaRoy Barley *(spelling per transcript/captions — not
in agenda)* of Raleigh, NC, speaking for the owners, said contractors who viewed the
house externally believe it can be saved, but the family stayed out because of the
unsafe-structure notice, and asked for time and interior access. Irwin: an
independent contractor rated it a 98% loss; the April 22 demolition deadline passed
with no action; individuals can't enter, but a licensed contractor can be arranged.
Kling floated giving Irwin discretion ("give him the hammer") vs. neighborhood blight
concerns; Meredith noted that would need a formal amendment. Consensus landed on
approving as-is: Public Works needs a minimum of ~30 days to mobilize, and if the
family's contractor produces a renovation budget and timeline in that window, staff
will consider it.

**Lodging tax postponed again (19a).** Hamilton: the +1% lodging tax (earmarked for
VBC capital improvements) and the TIF are two halves of the same funding strategy,
and the TIF vote is scheduled for June 25 — better to take both up the same night.
Battle: this follows through on the city's word to the Hotel/Motel Association, and
an October effective date gives hotels time to update collection systems.

**I-65 interchange study (20j).** Davis: the I-65/I-565 interchange is congested and
a full rebuild would run $300–400M. A cheaper alternative discussed with Federal
Highway (Mark Bartley) for about a year: a new ~$30–40M interchange between the
Huntsville–Browns Ferry exit and I-565, feeding the Mazda Toyota / western growth
corridor and eventually connecting toward Mooresville Rd, Greenbrier Pkwy, Hwy 31,
Calhoun Community College, and Pryor Field. The city likely won't build it, but wants
the location locked into ALDOT/FHWA systems before functional failure. Meredith
praised the progress.

**Northern Bypass retail (20i).** Davis: the Northern Bypass extension (Pulaski Pike
to ~1,500 ft east of Memorial Pkwy) wraps up later this year, and a major retail
development anchored by **Home Depot and Target** is going in mid-corridor — dirt
moves this summer, Target footings first, Home Depot out of the ground by late fall.
This design contract adds two signalized intersections and right-in/right-outs now,
so the new road doesn't open straight into orange barrels.

**Janitorial contract do-over (20c/20d).** The administrative-buildings janitorial
RFP went to a best-value winner that was also low bidder, but the awardee (Office
Pride) couldn't complete post-award requirements including a performance bond. Council
terminated and re-awarded to the second-place scorer, PJH Cleaning d/b/a Alabama
Cleaning Service — about $70K/yr more, but strong on all other metrics. The separate
parks-and-rec janitorial contract bids in ~6 months.

**Farm leases explained (20k/20n).** Prompted by "why so cheap?": Davis said lease
rates track yield — the $8,400 Cummings Research Park tract is unirrigated and the
$5,180 treatment-plant tract is a rocky former laydown area, while the irrigated
233-ac DeVaney tract at Greenbrier (consent item 20m, the Eli Lilly remnant) fetches
$29,150. Farming keeps marketable land maintained and show-ready for prospects;
engineering benchmarks rates against large local landowners every ~3 years.

**Travel expenses (20a).** Watkins asked why this period's travel ($30,291.76,
including $8,355.93 in employee reimbursements) was roughly double the prior
meeting's; staff: it varies with how many travel and how far (Detroit planning
conference, Decatur GA, other out-of-state). Robinson asked whether there's any
limit on a whole department attending the same conference — there isn't; it's the
department head's call, sometimes driven by continuing-education requirements.

**Other member notes.** Watkins: thanks to Fire, HPD, Chris McNeese/Public Works and
Shane Davis for fast response when Medaris Rd at Shepherd *(street names uncertain in
transcript — captions hear "Mederus Road in Shepherd"; verify)* flooded Sunday; free
Trevor Lacy basketball camp June 20, 9–12, John Hunt Park (ages 10–17); free District 1

streets (Holiday, Eustis, Cypress, Randolph, Harwood, Poinciana, Pansy, Town and
Country, Hillmont, Fennell *(spellings per transcript)*) have curbs poured but no

morning — money is already appropriated, it's a "when," not "if." Little: thanks to
Public Works, HPD, Traffic, Engineering and Inspections. Robinson: welcomed council
intern Amy Wallace and all city interns; June 27 Independence Day celebration at
Ditto Landing (gates 5 PM, free admission, $5/car cash parking, fireworks 9 PM)
kicks off the 250th-anniversary week ending with the July 4 downtown parade (~5 PM).
Battle and Meredith passed on comments.
