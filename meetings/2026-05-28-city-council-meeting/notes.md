## TL;DR

- **The lodging-tax ordinance (Ord 26-484) was introduced here** (item 21a) — first reading;
  no vote. It went on to be postponed June 11 and June 25 and finally passed July 9.
- **TIF 9 was only *certified* tonight (Res 26-462), not created.** Shane Davis stressed the
  district was *not* being created yet — this was a procedural certification under Ala. Code
  §11-99-4; creation required later action by both the city and Madison County.
- **97.24-acre Greenbrier Pkwy rezoning (C-4 → Residence 2) approved with Meredith voting NO**
  (Ord 26-302) — McKinley Homes' ~250+ single-family first phase of a larger mixed-use plan.
- **A liquor-license appeal failed for lack of a motion** — the Liquor License Review Committee's
  approval of Tena's Lounge (Bar Holdings LLC, Memorial Pkwy SE) therefore stands.
- **$34,627,133.42 in expenditures approved** (Res 26-455); Penny Smith gave a mid-year budget
  report (lodging tax running ~2.3% behind prior year; no rate changes proposed then).
- **Heavy public comment on library funding** (flat $5.6M for 3 years; staff no COLA in 2 years)
  and on **Sherwood Park roadway noise** — the latter produced Res 26-478 urging MPO/ALDOT action.

## Discussion highlights

**TIF 9 certification (20f).** Urban & Economic Development director **Shane Davis** pushed back on
"public confusion," stressing the council was **not creating TIF 9 tonight** — Alabama law requires a
sequence of steps by both the city and the Madison County Commission (the other voting authority).
The council had already asked for a project plan (brought the prior meeting); this item was the
certification under §11-99-4, with district creation to follow. *(A public commenter characterized
the plan as a "$220M TIF, $200M of it to the Von Braun Center" — see Public comments; that is the
speaker's framing, not staff's.)*

**Mid-year budget report (Finance).** Finance Director **Penny Smith** presented proposed mid-year
changes (adopted informally via the 20c budget amendment). Lodging-tax collections were running
**~2.3% behind the prior year and ~0.5% behind budget**; she proposed **no lodging-tax rate change**
at that time.

**Sherwood Park roadway noise (20g + public comment).** Multiple residents (see below) described
**Highway 255/Research Park noise and flying debris** — one recounted a tire crashing through a fence
into a child's play area. Kling's resolution asks the **Metropolitan Planning Organization to press
ALDOT** to study a sound-mitigation wall for Sherwood Park and Steeplechase residents. Passed
(second by Meredith).

**Greenbrier Pkwy rezoning (9e).** Planning manager **Thomas Nunez** explained the parcel has been
C-4 since annexation; the rezone to Residence 2 enables McKinley Homes' ~250+ single-family first
phase, with retail/mixed-use to follow along Greenbrier Pkwy. Resident **Joy Johnson** questioned
sprawl and C-4-vs-R2 density; **Meredith voted no.**

**Liquor-license appeal (9d).** City Attorney **Trey Riley** walked the council through an unusual
posture — an appeal of an *approval* (not the usual denial). Owner **Alex Legg** (Bar Holdings /
Tena's Lounge) said he'd done due diligence and there were no recent police issues. With **no motion
made**, the committee's approval stood.

**Presentations (7a/7b).** Library executive director **Connie Chow** and board vice-chair **Doug
Martinson** detailed the aging 40-year-old downtown branch (failing HVAC, steam boiler ~$65K/yr,
original fire/elevator systems, special-collections preservation risk) and asked for more funding;
**Singing River Trail** director **Misty Potter** reported growth to a 220-mile, 8-county plan, with
Meredith objecting that District 5/Huntsville greenways remain disconnected ("inside-out isn't
working for us").
