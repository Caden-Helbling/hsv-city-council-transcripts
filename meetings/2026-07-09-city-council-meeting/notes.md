## TL;DR

- **TIF 9 created** (Res 26-592) — a five-project tax-increment district (Von Braun
  Center North Hall expansion, North Huntsville/Beltline greenway, new Mill Creek Park
  affordable-housing project, Lowe Ave, new Huntsville Middle School, federal courthouse
  reno). Passed after the County Commission's unanimous vote last week; staff stressed
  repeatedly it is **not a tax increase**.
- **Lodging tax +1% approved** (Ord 26-484) after three postponements (introduced May 28,
  postponed June 11 and June 25) — the new penny is **dedicated to the Von Braun Civic
  Center** and was the piece linked to TIF 9.
- **$21,892,647.70 in expenditures approved** (Res 26-578).
- **$30,000 of District 3 improvement funds → fleet** for a Green Mountain volunteer
  fire truck (the VFD raised >$100K); city fleet budget covers the balance (Ord 26-580).
- **Ryan Renaud declared elected to the Huntsville school board (District 4)** unopposed
  (Res 26-593, term begins Nov 2, 2026) — taken out of order early in the meeting.
- Two nuisance actions approved (6026 Lincoya Dr abatement; 3208 Tenth Ave SW demolition,
  a 9-year-unsafe, 83%-loss structure).
- Everything passed by **voice vote; no roll-call splits or abstentions were recorded**.

## Discussion highlights

**TIF District 9 (20g).** Urban & Economic Development director **Shane Davis** presented the
district's five projects: Von Braun Center North Hall expansion, North Huntsville/Beltline
greenway, the new **Mill Creek Park** (a Choice Neighborhood affordable-residential project
with the Huntsville Housing Authority), Lowe Avenue improvements, a new **Huntsville Middle
School** (with HCS), and federal courthouse renovations for the County Commission's future
use. Kling walked Davis through a deliberate **"is this a tax increase?" — "No, sir"** exchange
for the public: the city can't raise millage without a referendum and doesn't assess property
(the county does); a TIF only captures future assessed-value growth inside the boundary to pay
for the projects, after which the district expires and proceeds flow to the taxing entities.
Davis noted the ~58 total mills, with the **6.5 state mills and the 5.5-mill county-wide
educational tax not captured**. The County Commission approved the plan unanimously last week;
legal descriptions go to the tax assessor the next morning.

**Lodging tax (19a).** Finance's **Penny Smith** *(spelling per transcript — "Pennie")*
explained the amendment adds **1% to the lodging tax** (currently 9% local + $2/night service
charge), with that penny **dedicated to Von Braun Civic Center repairs, maintenance, and
expansion**. It had been held pending the TIF 9 vote and passed the same night.

**Vendor gratuity policy (19b).** Meredith ("son of an English teacher") moved a set of
corrections before passage: "Code of Ordinances" made plural, striking a stray "not" in
section C ("shall **not** promise"), and removing two commas in section D. Council adopted the
amendment, then the ordinance as amended.

**Green Mountain fire truck (14c).** The chair (Robinson) explained the transfer of **$30,000**
from District 3 improvement funds back into the capital plan / fleet services to supplement the
**>$100,000 the Green Mountain volunteer fire association raised** for a specialized truck able
to navigate the mountain's long (~400-ft) driveways and carry its own water; the item also seeds
the inaugural **Summer Haze** community event.

**HPCAC annual report (7a).** Board chair **Becky Schmidt** (with vice-chair Dan Odom, secretary
Ryan O'Connor, member Missy Hanks; Chief Giles present) reported three public meetings this year
— the internal-affairs session drew a crowd, the listening session and elder-fraud session much
less. New contact address **hpcacconnect@gmail.com**. In Q&A, Watkins pressed on **how the
council measures community trust** (Schmidt: no statistical metric yet — they review quarterly IA
summaries; a resident survey is planned) and offered to host HPCAC at council town halls; Meredith
argued IA complaints are a skewed sample and urged working with **community watch groups and CROs**
for a truer read.

**Northern Bypass change order (20k).** Davis framed CO#3 as a rare **"good" (negative) change
order** — a $31,440 reduction for storm drain that wasn't needed — and said the bypass is on track
to have **all lanes open by year-end**, with the Mount Lebanon → Toyota segment the heavy lift
remaining.

**NIBIN/NESS MOU (20y).** At Watkins's request (anticipating "an email in the morning talking about
ICE"), an HPD administrative-services manager explained **NIBIN** is a national ballistics network
— every firearm taken in is test-fired and the casing catalogued to match guns across crimes/cities.
Confirmed on the record: **no ICE involvement and no cost.**

**Huntsville Salutes America's 250th (5a).** Mayor Battle recognized **Sally Warden** and the
committee/partners (Historic Huntsville Foundation, CVB, Chamber, Alabama A&M, library/DAR, Parks &
Rec, Downtown Huntsville Inc., and title sponsor **Meta**, whose Henry Thornton spoke) for the July 4
celebration — an estimated **15,000+ attendees**; WHNT broadcast the parade live.
