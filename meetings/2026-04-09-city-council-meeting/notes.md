## Discussion highlights

**40 MW solar lease (20R).** Shane Davis (Urban & Economic Development) + Chris Jones (Huntsville Utilities
COO) presented a four-party ground lease — City, Madison County (co-landlords of a ~30-yr-held 237-ac site
near 6th St & Wall Triana), Huntsville Utilities, and EE Gemini Solar LLC (a Eurus Inc. entity, selected via
competitive RFP). 35-year term; the land is leased, not sold, and reverts to natural state if not renewed.
$23,796 partial rent during the 14-month build, then **$237,690/yr split 50/50 with the county**. TVA's
recent policy change lets its cooperatives self-generate; HU expects the purchased solar energy to cost less
than TVA power with ~2-year payback on the new substation — **no rate impact**.

**Police recruiting & staffing (20P).** John Hamilton framed Crimson Recruiting as the start of a broader
push: the department has **~75 authorized vacancies**, academy capacity is ~35/class but recent classes have
graduated **8–12**, and only ~10–20 of several hundred applicants per cycle clear the bar. The firm succeeded
in Mobile on marketing to the "right" candidate profile; most return is expected locally/regionally.
Compensation studies and further items are coming.

**DEA cooperative agreement (20N).** Watkins pressed twice on whether the renewal touches ICE — Hamilton:
"It does not," and it involves no geographic targeting; it's a North Alabama regional drug task force. Kling
added that HPD's own reports show drug activity is citywide, not concentrated in one district.

**Cable/utility clutter (Kling + City Attorney Riley).** Kling raised rusted, knocked-over, and abandoned
cable/fiber boxes and dangling wires across neighborhoods. Riley explained the city's 2020 police-power
ordinance for right-of-way communication facilities and why its tools (municipal-court prosecution, stop-work
orders, permit revocation) are weak against corporate providers; the city leans on a cooperative ITS-led
effort via SeeClickFix and is exploring **injunctive relief in circuit court** where public safety or repeat
violations are implicated.

**Crestwood/Huntsville Hospital (Kling).** Huntsville Hospital's CEO (Kling: "Mr. Sams" — *Jeff Samz,
spelling per transcript*) will present on the Crestwood acquisition at the **April 23** meeting given
community interest.

**ParkZen (20M).** Keisha Bryant: a hardware-free app giving real-time downtown parking availability +
wayfinding; $10,000 to geofence city lots/garages, $15,000/yr subscription, free to users. Robinson noted
the perennial "no parking downtown" complaint the app aims to answer.
