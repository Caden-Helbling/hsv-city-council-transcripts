## Discussion highlights

**Personnel Hearing (Item 8a):**
While the Council voted to uphold the Personnel Committee's decision regarding Calvin Hadden (Huntsville Fire Department), Councilmember Bill Kling requested specific follow-up actions. He asked that the City Administrator and City Attorney review the personnel manual's definition of "retaliation" and directives regarding special duty assignments. Specifically, he requested the deletion of section 14.2A1, which currently states the HR Director's decision on grievances is final. City Administrator John Hamilton confirmed staff would review these areas and report back to the Council at the first meeting in October.

**Paracycling World Championships (Item 7a):**
Claire Aiello of the Chamber of Commerce presented details for the upcoming event (Sept 4-7, 2026). She highlighted a projected economic impact of **$4.1 million** and the arrival of 50 countries. A major point of discussion was the need for 160 volunteer shifts for course marshaling, specifically for the road races on Sept 6-7. Aiello emphasized traffic safety, noting athletes will train on public roads starting Aug 25, and urged the public to be cautious.

**Tax Increment Financing (TIF D8) (Items 10a, 20G, 20H):**
Shane Davis, Director of Urban Economic Development, clarified that TIF D8 is inextricably linked to the upcoming Eli Lilly campus investment at Exit 3. He explained that the district encompasses approximately **9,054.82 acres** and is necessary to fund public infrastructure (roads, utilities) to support the new jobs and industry. He noted that while the district was reserved years ago, the boundary is being finalized now. A public hearing is set for Sept 10, 2026, to discuss the project plan.

**Orion Amphitheater Parking (Item 20O):**
Ricky Wilkinson, Director of General Services, presented the contract with Thrash Commercial Contractors for a new parking deck. The **$32,950,000** contract includes a four-story deck with 924 spaces. Councilmember Little clarified that while intended for the amphitheater, the deck will be open to the public for free parking during normal business hours for the Mid-City area.

**Graham Farms Mixed-Use Development (Item 20Y):**
Shane Davis detailed the **300-acre** project at the intersection of Greenbrier Parkway and Mooresville Road. The master plan includes 1,050 traditional single-family lots, 100 active adult (55+) lots, 85 townhomes, 30 live-work flats, 630 multifamily units, and 255,000 sq. ft. of commercial/retail. The project aims to provide diverse housing price points to maintain Huntsville's affordability amidst the Eli Lilly expansion. The developer must complete 50% of Phase 1 lots (175 lots) and have 20 homes under construction before the city provides sewer and road infrastructure.
