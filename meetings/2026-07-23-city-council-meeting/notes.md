## TL;DR

- **Harris Farms approved (9e, Ord 26-495):** 505 acres at Bob Wade Ln / Mt. Lebanon Rd
  rezoned to Planned Development–Housing — **~1,800 homes** (30/40/54-ft products +
  141 townhomes), ~350 ac open space, with major dimensional modifications
  (63% lot coverage vs the normal 40%). No one spoke at the public hearing.
- **Northern Bypass (SR 255) is 75% complete and targeted to open by end of 2026**;
  FHWA just agreed to let Phase 2 (US 231 → Winchester Rd) bid next year. Adjacent
  **North Village Town Center phase 1 is 100% sold out** — Target and Home Depot
  closings in Aug–Sept, opening 2027; Huntsville Hospital breaks ground on a ~$50M
  ER/imaging center there next year.
- **$30,015,087.43 in expenditures approved** (14a, Res 26-623).
- **August 25 municipal election machinery locked in:** 225 poll workers across 26
  precincts, 43 voting machines + 6 spares from Madison County (~$76,930), and the
  ES&S ballots/support quote (~$37,000).
- **Same-night addition 20ad passed:** emergency lease of a Brindley Mountain ladder
  truck at **$360/day** because multiple HFD ladder trucks are down with long-lead
  maintenance — couldn't wait three weeks for the next meeting.
- **Both Museum Board reappointments postponed to Aug 13** — they need a 4/5 vote and
  Little was absent.
- **Providence traffic boiled over:** Meredith presented slides of delivery trucks
  blocking the west side's only entrance; a resident demanded five specific fixes.
  A second entrance is "a few months away."
- A resident challenged the city on **Flock ALPR transparency**; Meredith announced his
  **next town hall will be about the Flock cameras**.

## Discussion highlights

**Northern Bypass update (7a, Shane Davis — requested by Watkins).** SR 255 extension:
4.5 miles of limited-access highway, Pulaski Pike to ~1,000 ft east of US 231/431,
original cost $43M, replacing two-lane Bob Wade Lane. **75% complete; contract date
Nov 2026**, staff pushing to open by end of calendar year. Crews are in the hardest
section — busting rock along the mountain toe; the slope turned out "mixed face" (part
dirt, part rock) instead of solid rock, so a supplemental stabilization plan (slope
paving or riprap, ALDOT's call) is being designed but shouldn't hold up opening. When
done it completes a **14-mile limited-access loop from I-565 to US 231**; modeling says
it cuts ~15 minutes off north-county commutes to Redstone/Research Park and diverts
**~15,000 cars/day** from the I-565/US 231 interchange (a ~$350M rebuild someday).
The remaining two-lane Bob Wade Lane stub must be renamed — the Sharp family (long-time
residents) floated as an option, council's call. **Phase 2 (US 231 → Winchester Rd**
*(transcript garbles the cross road — likely Moores Mill; verify)***) is at 60% plans**;
after a year of advocacy, FHWA agreed ~2 weeks ago to a logical terminus at Winchester
rather than forcing the ~$180M run to US 72 — **bid expected next year**. Eastern
bypass (Hampton Cove → US 72): corridor study done, ALDOT/FHWA tie-in approved this
year, design funds already in the CIP for next year. Kling pitched his long-term "ring
road" vision; staff joked about calling it the "Kling Ring."

**North Village Town Center (Davis, same presentation).** 200-acre mixed-use development
at US 231/SR 255: 650,000+ sq ft of retail/restaurant/medical/hospitality. Utility
relocations ~95% done; **real-estate closings with Target and Home Depot in August–
September**, tilt-up walls this fall, **phase 1 opens 2027** and is **100% sold out**
(~350,000 sq ft, "best-in-class household names"). Huntsville Hospital closed on its
phase-2 parcel — ~$50M emergency & imaging center breaking ground next year. Phase 3
land (toward Mt. Lebanon Rd) in final negotiations; ties into tonight's Harris Farms
rezoning. New Camden Place single-family subdivision already under construction.

**Harris Farms rezoning (9e, Thomas Nunez).** 505.03 ac → PDH, LUI 45 & 56: ~1,800
units — 548 30-ft-product homes, 623 40-ft, 226 54-ft, 141 front-loaded townhomes —
**~350 ac open space + 15 ac recreation** (pickleball, greenways, dog parks, pocket
parks), 6-ft sidewalks (code requires 4). Modifications: 42-ft/5,040 sq ft minimum lots
(vs 50-ft/6,000), 15-ft rear setbacks, 5-ft sides (5.5-ft two-story, 12-ft sum),
**lot coverage 40% → 63%**, townhome front garages, 45-ft amenity buildings, 24-ft
pavement in a 50-ft ROW. Developer team: Suncrest / Thomas & Hutton *(other firm names
garbled in transcript)*. Watkins explained her yes vote: she'd previously voted against
a Meredith-district development over school coordination, but Davis, Battle, and HCS
Superintendent Dr. Sutton personally reassured her the school system is on board.
Battle framed it as the final stage of the north-Huntsville progression: jobs →
infrastructure → retail → residential.

**Emergency ladder truck (20ad, Hamilton).** Multiple HFD ladder trucks are down at
once with long-lead-time parts; Brindley Mountain Fire Apparatus (local) happens to
have trucks available. $360/day lease for several months — cheap against a $2M+
purchase; added same-night because such trucks "do not come along very often."

**California Park trees (Kling ↔ Hamilton).** Constituents alarmed by tree removal at
the Huntsville Middle School construction site (HCS property adjacent to city-owned
California Park). Hamilton: all school work is on school land; **California Park is
untouched**, and a park renovation project will appear in the capital-plan update he
brings in September, with public input before anything is built.

**Mayor Battle (fresh off the plane).** ~20 meetings at the London air show with
companies looking to relocate/expand, several moving production from overseas; announced
**150 jobs ≈ $12M/yr payroll** for Huntsville. Weekend: CAFY back-to-school event at
Calhoun, Times Building 100th anniversary, Delta Sigma Theta luncheon.

**Recycling failure (Robinson).** Recycling pickups slipped badly after July 4 — her
own cart sat 10 days ("inexcusable"). Publicly told the Solid Waste Disposal Authority
**"we need a new contractor for our recycling"**; thanked city sanitation crews who
worked extra hours covering the contractor's shortfall.

**Providence traffic (Meredith, with slides).** Delivery trucks routinely block the
west side's **only entrance** (Old Broadway area). HPD/traffic enforcement stepping up;
a **second entrance to west Providence is "a few months away"** and also improves
fire/rescue access. (See public comment #2 for the resident version.)

**Other member notes.** Meredith: condolences on the death of **Tom Glenn** (senior
center leader; was expanding Meals on Wheels into District 5) — echoed by Kling;
WeFeel Foundation backpack event Sat July 25, West Huntsville Rec Center; ribbon
cutting at the Grove at Indian Creek senior affordable housing (Jeff Rd). Watkins:
