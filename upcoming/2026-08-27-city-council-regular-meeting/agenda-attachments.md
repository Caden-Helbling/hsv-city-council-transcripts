        WHEREAS, the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development
(“HUD”) has awarded the City of Huntsville (the “City”) Community Development Block Grant
(“CDBG”) funds, including PY25 and PY26 CDBG Economic Development funds designated
for the Mill Creek CDBG Façade Improvement Grant Program (the “Program”); and
        WHEREAS, the City, through its Community Development Department, issued
Invitation for Bid No. 67-2026-70 to competitively procure a single General Contractor to
perform turnkey façade improvement construction activities at multiple properties within the
Mill Creek Redevelopment Area on behalf of the Program; and
       WHEREAS, Strozier Construction Company, Inc. submitted a responsive and
responsible bid under Invitation for Bid No. 67-2026-70 and is the recommended awardee; and
        WHEREAS, the City desires to enter into a General Contractor Services Agreement with
Strozier Construction Company, Inc. to establish the terms under which façade improvement
construction services will be performed under the Program, with individual properties to be
separately authorized by project-specific Tripartite Construction Agreements among the City, the
General Contractor, and the applicable Subrecipient;
       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of the City of
Huntsville, Alabama, that the Mayor of the City of Huntsville is hereby authorized, requested,
and directed to execute a General Contractor Services Agreement between the City of Huntsville
and Strozier Construction Company, Inc., said agreement being substantially similar in words
and figures to that document identified as “Agreement Between the City of Huntsville, Alabama
and Strozier Construction Company, Inc.,” consisting of nineteen (19) pages, and the date of
August 27, 2026 appearing on the margin of the first page, together with the signature of the
President or President Pro Tem of the City Council, an executed copy of said document being
permanently kept on file in the Office of the City Clerk of the City of Huntsville.

                   THIS AGREEMENT, entered into this the 27th day of August, 2026, between the City
            of Huntsville, Alabama, hereinafter referred to as the Grantee, and Strozier Construction
            Company, Inc., hereinafter referred to as the Contractor.

                    WHEREAS, the City has received Community Development Block Grant funds under
            Assistance Listing Number 14.218, known as Grant No. B-25-MC-01-0005 and B-26-MC-01-
            0005, from the United States Government pursuant to Title I of the Housing and Community
            Development Act of 1974, as amended (HCD Act), Public Law 93-383; and

                   WHEREAS, the City wishes to engage the Contractor to render certain services in
            connection therewith:

                     NOW THEREFORE, it is agreed between the parties hereto that;

            I.       SCOPE OF SERVICE

                     A.      Activities

            The Contractor shall, in a satisfactory and proper manner, as determined by the City of
            Huntsville Community Development Department, provide services in accordance with Invitation
            for Bid No. 50-2026-70, issued June 24, 2026 (the “IFB”). The Contractor's bid response
            provides for turnkey General Contractor services for multiple façade improvement projects
            within the Mill Creek Redevelopment Area, on an as-needed basis, under the Mill Creek CDBG
            Façade Improvement Grant Program (the “Program”). The Program is funded through HUD
            CDBG funds and is intended to revitalize commercial corridors, improve building safety and
            accessibility, enhance exterior aesthetics, and support small businesses and eligible entities
            within the Mill Creek Redevelopment Area.

            Individual projects shall not exceed $24,999.00 in CDBG-assisted cost and shall require a 10%
            match from the Subrecipient (property owner/business). The Contractor shall develop scopes of
            work for each awarded project, including site visits, feasibility assessments, itemized cost
            estimates, and identification of code and ADA issues. All scopes must be approved by the City
            prior to execution of a Tripartite Construction Agreement or commencement of construction.

            Eligible Activities: Exterior improvements including painting, windows/doors, lighting,
            masonry repair, awnings, and code compliance improvements.
